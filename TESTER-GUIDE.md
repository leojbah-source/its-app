below in a normal web browser (Chrome is best). Phone, tablet, or computer are
all fine.

> about 30–60 seconds to wake up on the first visit. After that it's quick.

## What to try

You don't have to test everything — poke around and note anything confusing or
broken. Some useful things to check:

**Public board (`/pwa`)**
- Do the **Results**, **Schedule**, and **Awards** tabs open and show sensible
  information?
- On a phone, does everything fit the screen and read clearly?

**Participant login (`/pwa/login`)**
- Sign in as a participant. Do **My Results** and **My Schedule** look right?
- Are the point totals at the top believable?

**Admin (`/admin/login`)**
- Look through the menus: Events, Registrations, Schedule, Event Day, Judging.
- In **Judging → Results**, pick an event and age group. Try **Compute**, and
  the **Print sheet** button (it should open a clean sheet you could print).

**Judge (`/judge/login`)**
- Sign in with the judge phone number. Read the briefing, then try entering some
  scores. Does anything block you or behave oddly?

**MC / Timer**
- Do the stage script and the stopwatch screens make sense for someone standing
  at an event?

## How to report a problem

For anything that looks wrong, jot down:

1. **Where** you were (which link / screen).
2. **What you did** (what you clicked or typed).
3. **What happened** vs what you expected.
4. A **screenshot** if you can (very helpful).

Send those back to Leo. Small notes are perfect — even "this word is confusing"
is useful. Thank you!
